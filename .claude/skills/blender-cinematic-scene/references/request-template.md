# Reusable modeling brief

Replace the bracketed fields before sending this brief to an agent.

> Create an extremely detailed, realistic Blender project of [subject and exact version]. Deliver [editable model / stills / animation / interactive exploration].
>
> Research trustworthy photographs and videos where fidelity matters. Verify the subject, organize references by component and room, and distinguish confirmed features from inferred details. Plan the complete exterior, interior and connections logically before adding fine detail.
>
> Prioritize substantial modeled depth: thickness, recesses, frames, seams, bevels, fasteners, vents, cables, seals, mounts and working mechanisms where appropriate. Avoid accidental holes, floating parts, blocked passages and arbitrary decorative clutter.
>
> Use convincing layered PBR materials and strong cinematic lighting. I prefer exaggerated but believable contrast, atmosphere and material response over flat lighting or plain colored surfaces. Keep important details readable and use volumetrics only where they fit the environment.
>
> Optimize for [my actual hardware, or inspect it]. For exploration, aim for around 20 FPS or better, but visual quality matters more than a strict minimum. Measure moving performance honestly and preserve nearby geometry at every viewing height.
>
> If exploration is requested, provide WASD movement, arrow-key viewing, Space/Shift vertical movement and a speed modifier. Add [requested interactive doors or animations] with documented keys and complete transitions, including return behavior.
>
> Write and execute the Blender code. Fix errors, inspect real renders of the exterior and all major rooms, and verify geometry, lighting, controls and animation. Deliver the blend, reproducible scripts, required assets, preview instructions and representative renders.
>
> Work autonomously and ask only necessary questions. State remaining approximations and untested behavior. Do not call an unfinished or unexecuted model complete.
