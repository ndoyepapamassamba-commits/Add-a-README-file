# Quality and delivery

## Export from the brief

Define each deliverable's destination, native dimensions, aspect ratio, frame rate, duration, codec/container or image format, color transform, captions, and audio before final production. Consult the destination's current official guidance at export time. A sample preset or previous film is not a platform rule. Do not silently upscale or frame-interpolate material that the brief calls native.

For portrait versions, compose for the actual crop and interface safe areas, and edit the story to fit the requested duration. Do not accelerate a long horizontal cut simply to fit another platform. For stills, verify intended color profile, dimensions, alpha/background, and file format.

## Technical and visual checks

- Check exact frame sequence coverage and order, including the first and last retained frames and every chunk or cut boundary.
- Probe the exported file for dimensions, frame rate behavior, pixel aspect, duration, codec, color tags, and audio streams. Decode the complete file, not just the opening seconds.
- Compare color-transformed output against representative raw/finished frames. Metadata tags cannot repair a wrong pixel transform.
- Make contact sheets at useful intervals to spot jumps in camera, lighting, material, text, and action. Inspect suspicious sections as motion, because a contact sheet cannot reveal flicker, pacing, sync, or sound.
- Watch every final film at normal speed and listen to its complete mix. Check narration wording, intelligibility, pronunciation, sound/music balance, clipping, silence, transitions, and ending. If media playback is unavailable, state that the visual or audio review remains open and provide playable files.
- For a still, inspect at final display size and full resolution. Review focus, edges, materials, text, crop, color, and source fidelity.

## Handoff

Retain the editable Blender and Unreal projects, required external assets, source/shot manifests, selected render settings, original audio and stems when available, final outputs, rights/attribution notes, and concise recovery steps. Use the user's selected destination. Verify file existence and integrity there; a queued sync or local export is not verified delivery.

Report which checks passed, failed, were unavailable, or were not run. Distinguish technical validation, agent visual review, delivery, and the user's acceptance. Publishing or sending to an external audience is a separate action unless the request authorizes it.
