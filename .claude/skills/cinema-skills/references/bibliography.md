# 📚 Bibliography

A reading list behind the skills in this repository: well-known books on
screenwriting, directing, cinematography, editing, color, sound, VFX and
production, grouped by discipline.

**148 books** · only books whose title, author(s) and year are well established
are listed — nothing is included on a guess.

## ⚖️ Copyright policy — read before writing a skill

- **No verbatim text from these books anywhere in this repository.** No quotes,
  no excerpts, no epigraphs, no tables or diagrams copied or redrawn, no
  chapter-by-chapter paraphrase, no cover images or page scans.
- **Skills contain original summaries of principles and techniques.** Ideas,
  methods and craft techniques are not protected by copyright — their
  expression is. Explain a principle in your own words, apply it to our
  pipeline, and credit the book by *title, author and year* in the skill's
  `Sources` section.
- **The same rule applies to other people's skills, scripts, films and
  manuals.** Link to them; do not copy them, unless their license allows it and
  the attribution it requires is kept.
- **Want the full text? Buy or borrow the book.** This list exists to point
  people to the authors.

*Year* is the year of first publication; for translated works the first
English edition is noted. Later editions sometimes change subtitles or add
co-authors (noted where it matters).
